# Portfolio extraction

Updated October 1, 2026.

The engine extracts source-backed facts rather than estimating company size. Managed apartments, residences, homes and units are residential counts; retail/commercial square footage and homes developed or built are separate facts. Qualifiers such as “over,” “approximately” and “+” remain intact. Adjacent number/label blocks on official pages are preserved without attaching a neighboring development counter to a managed-residences label.

Only identity-matched company evidence is eligible. Customer portfolio claims, transaction/pipeline figures and title-only counts do not establish the company's managed portfolio. Residential counts are preferred over property counts. Non-historical evidence outranks historical evidence; dates and source rank resolve subsequent ordering. Undated evidence is not proof of recency. Different reported totals remain visible for review with their quotes and sources; the engine does not select the largest number or silently combine scopes.

Finding housing fit alone does not end research when a portfolio count is still missing. Observed official overview, management and portfolio links are preferred within the existing request/time limits. No paid tier or higher provider allowance is introduced.

Existing retained evidence is re-extracted when a lead is presented. Incomplete older extracts can request a bounded refresh. Complete cached page evidence is reused; old incomplete page extracts receive a versioned, cached retry to recover discarded statistic blocks. Retrieval dates are preserved. Refreshing a lead can still reuse fresh cache.

Regression fixtures include the 130,000 apartments / 4 million retail square feet sentence, 140,000+ residences managed alongside 62,000 developed/acquired/built homes, conflicting dated totals, and misleading customer counts. These are parsing fixtures, not hardcoded live company facts. Fit and priority rules are unchanged, and portfolio totals are not automatically inserted into outreach.

Limitations: websites may be blocked, incomplete or stale. Unsupported layouts can still be missed. Sources and human review remain necessary; the engine does not guarantee an exhaustive or current portfolio inventory.
