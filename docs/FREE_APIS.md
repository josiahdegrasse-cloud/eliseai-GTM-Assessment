# Free research choices

Updated October 1, 2026: public keyless endpoints remain the default. The owner may explicitly configure `JINA_FREE_API_KEY` as a server secret to use a finite free token allowance. Legacy EXA_API_KEY and JINA_API_KEY remain ignored; no billing or top-up API is called. Free services can throttle or be unavailable; hosting/account subscription terms are separate.

## Active integrations

| Source | Job in the brief | Cost and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Jina Reader](https://jina.ai/reader/) | Read official company pages anonymously, using observed links and public overview paths. | Anonymous by default; optional dedicated free key stays server-side and is sent only to fixed Jina service endpoints with redirects disabled. Up to four company-page requests per lookup. Optional domain-restricted discovery uses the same reserved free allowance. |
| Direct official company pages | Company recovery and individual role/responsibility evidence. | Public DNS and robots checks, bounded same-domain HTTPS retrieval; no credentials, AI model, paid search or third-party person enrichment. |
| [Census geocoder](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html) | Address match, county and tract for the submitted U.S./Puerto Rico property. | Public endpoint, no key used. Address-range match is not building, ownership or unit-count verification. |
| [Census Reporter](https://github.com/censusreporter/census-api/blob/master/API.md) | ACS five-year tract estimates: gross rent, renter share, housing mix and rent cost burden. | Keyless API, independently operated. Actual dataset period and rent margin of error are retained; area aggregates never become property claims. |
| [USGS NAIP imagery](https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer) | Aerial view around the approximate submitted address, loaded on visibility. | Public-domain imagery, no key or billing account; conterminous U.S. coverage, variable image date. Google satellite / Street View links are keyless external navigation. |
| [wsrv.nl](https://wsrv.nl/faq/) | Optional company-logo image cache/resizer; converts an observed brand image to a bounded PNG. | Free service, no key. Best-effort availability; documented uncached-image IP limits apply. The app retains initials on failure. |
| [GLEIF](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-api) | Optional legal name, LEI, entity status and reported headquarters. | Public keyless lookup; coverage is businesses with LEI records. Accept one exact normalized legal-name match only. This remains an unverified candidate for the submitted business. |

The original source URLs and retrieval dates stay visible. Figures retain qualifiers and scope. Missing facts stay unknown. GLEIF never changes the fit category or email; a missing registry match does not stop either workflow. Its LEI registration status is distinct from the legal entity's operating status.

Company research requests readable Markdown only. Requesting Markdown plus full HTML caused observed 409 BudgetExceededError responses (82,412 intended tokens on one page versus 4,688 for Markdown); the request remains capped at 20,000 tokens. Logo discovery reuses existing candidate metadata or performs one separately limited direct-HTML homepage fetch with a five-second timeout; it makes zero Reader requests. Missing branding cannot block company classification. Logo work yields to research and runs one at a time; it never adds buying-fit evidence. Company results and official company-page discovery are reused for up to seven days; Census and registry results for 24 hours. Repeated contacts at one company share a lookup within the visitor workspace. Only the explicitly published sample companies/addresses can share caches across visitors. Company lookups use public domains. Individual research can send the submitted name and employer to configured research providers; email bodies and private rep notes are not used as web research queries.

Direct company recovery follows up to three pages within a 12-second deadline, prioritizing observed company and investor links. Navigation labels guide discovery but cannot become factual evidence. Account briefs retain distinct sourced ownership, operating approach, team/headquarters and housing-focus statements, plus separate home and property totals when available. These facts do not establish buyer intent.

Company Reader has 60 attempts per visitor per UTC day; Census/GLEIF retain 30 each. Individual research has 60 bounded lookups per visitor per day. Reader starts share eight-second global pacing and provider cooldowns. Company lookups also enforce eight attempts per minute globally. Limits are abuse controls and do not override upstream restrictions. Saved evidence and edited emails survive failures; repeated company lookups reuse session-private caches. With the explicit free key, the company Reader limits become 30/minute and 300/workspace/day, with two-second global pacing. Company, contact, search and embedding calls share the same token ledger when `JINA_TOKEN_ACCOUNTING=1`. Initialization reads `https://dash.jina.ai/api/v1/authorization` with the key in an Authorization header. It requires a valid free trial balance, no purchased balance, no auto-recharge flag and no saved payment method. It reserves at most 8M tokens from the verified remaining free balance, leaving at least 100k untouched. This ceiling does not renew. Every request atomically reserves 20k tokens; a successful response with a numeric token-usage field settles exactly once. Missing usage or a failed request retains the full reservation. Other applications can consume the same key’s balance, so the displayed app allowance is not a live provider balance. Provider 402 responses still stop the request. No billing mutations are made. The legacy 400-attempt ledger stays intact and remains the fallback configuration when the rollout setting is off.

## Location handling

Company addresses come from organization-scoped PostalAddress metadata or explicitly labeled headquarters/corporate-office text on the supplied company website. Contact and office-directory links are followed only when observed on that website. Research remains capped at four pages and a bounded crawl window, and uses the same page cache as logos. Up to three distinct addresses are shown with the original source and retrieval date. An office address is never copied into the submitted property or geocoded as that property. GLEIF headquarters remain separately labeled legal-name candidates.

The Census geocoder accepts either separate street/city/state, street plus ZIP, or a full pasted address through its documented onelineaddress endpoint. After an explicit no-match, one alternate full-address lookup can omit apartment/suite details. Ambiguous matches and provider failures never trigger a guessed pin. Normalized returned addresses, county and source remain visible. This remains an approximate address-range match, not parcel verification.

References: [Census address formats](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/geocoder/Geocoding_Services_API.html), [Schema.org PostalAddress](https://schema.org/PostalAddress), [Google Maps search URLs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/guide).

Area requests send only a validated Census tract identifier and fixed housing-table IDs to `api.censusreporter.org`. Results are cached within the visitor session with seven-day nominal freshness, bounded by the session’s 24-hour retention. Limits: 60 requests per visitor per UTC day and 30 global attempts per minute. Direct Census Data calls currently require a free key; the keyless geocoder is a separate service.


## Rate-limit prevention

Company research is reused for up to 24 hours, with its original retrieval timestamp. This trades intraday freshness for fewer calls; new company identities still require research. Contacts with the same company name/domain reuse the same visitor cache. Existing privacy boundaries remain: arbitrary visitor companies are not shared across workspaces.

In anonymous mode the site permits at most eight company Reader attempts per minute, with eight seconds between Reader starts across Worker instances. Free-key mode uses the separate limits described above. Repeated Reader 429s add persistent, provider-wide backoff: approximately 1, 2, 4, 8 and 15 minutes, with up to ten seconds of jitter; a longer Retry-After wins (bounded at 24 hours). This state survives tab reloads. One request at a time tests recovery after a cooldown. A successful response clears the failure streak unless a newer cooldown is active. All external attempts, including rejected requests, still count against daily quotas.

The research crawl still prioritizes observed corporate/investor links and can use up to four pages when needed. Page content and location candidates come from readable research text. Logo metadata uses a separate bounded direct fetch when needed. We have not removed useful company/location coverage simply to reduce the request count.

Anonymous Reader is used when no dedicated free key is configured. The optional free-key path requires explicit owner configuration, enforces the permanent reservation cap, and never buys more credits. Saved research and direct website access remain available if the free-key cap or provider allowance is exhausted. Paid Exa benchmark results remain historical.

## Hybrid fallback (September 30, 2026)

Jina Search and `jina-embeddings-v3` are enabled only with the explicit free key. They share Reader's verified, non-renewing free-token allowance; discovery and ranking have no separate credits. Keyword ranking runs locally. Semantic similarity ranks exact source passages and cannot establish identity, job authority, property ownership or housing fit. Search URLs must stay on the submitted company domain; search snippets never enter briefs or drafts. Results are cached privately for 24 hours; failures retain keyword evidence. See the README and this document for input bounds and limits.


### Direct fallback and allowance visibility

The token-accounting release replaces the production 400-attempt cutoff with the verified token ledger above; legacy counters are not erased. Exhaustion is checked before Reader pacing or network calls. Direct public website research remains independent. Run log displays the current mode and remaining app token allowance, explicitly distinguished from the provider’s current balance. Successful direct research names that retrieval method.

Direct company research is bounded to three pages and 12 seconds per lead, 12 page reservations per minute globally and 90 per workspace per UTC day. The prior 30-page daily limit could stop a 30-lead batch after roughly ten three-page attempts. The increase supports at most 30 worst-case company attempts per day, not guaranteed successful results. Cache hits do not reserve another page. Robots rules, public DNS checks, same-domain redirects and response bounds remain enforced. Person research has separate existing limits. No paid connector, top-up, news service or new secret is added.

Integration tests cover a cold lead with no key and anonymous Reader throttled, an exhausted app allowance (zero Reader network requests), and a provider quota response. Each must recover supported company facts and a draft through direct public HTML. Existing blocked-site, privacy, provenance and retained-draft tests remain required. These fixture tests show fallback logic, not universal access to websites.

### Token accounting validation

Tests cover free-only balance verification, concurrent reservations, idempotent refunds, unknown/failed usage, over-budget reports, paid/auto-recharging wallet rejection, preserved legacy counters and a cold lead completing through Reader after the legacy 400-attempt cutoff. The balance endpoint and wallet/usage shapes are based on [Jina’s Reader source](https://github.com/jina-ai/reader/blob/main/src/3rd-party/jina-embeddings.ts) and its [usage reporting](https://github.com/jina-ai/reader/blob/main/src/api/crawler.ts). These interfaces can change; an invalid response fails closed to direct public research.

## Optional Tavily: free-only search and page extraction

Added October 1, 2026. This expands the existing public-source pipeline without changing scoring weights or treating search summaries as facts. Jina/public retrieval remains available when Tavily is disconnected, throttled, exhausted or rejected.

- **Setup:** create a dedicated [Tavily Researcher account](https://app.tavily.com) with no paid plan and pay-as-you-go disabled. Store its key as the Sites server secret `TAVILY_FREE_API_KEY`, then deploy. Do not put the key in this repository, browser code or an intake field. The owner supplied a key on October 1, 2026. The Researcher plan, 1,000-credit allowance and disabled pay-as-you-go were verified. New free accounts return explicit null for the disabled pay-as-you-go cap and an unset per-key cap; the latter inherits the mandatory free account ceiling. Missing fields still reject.
- **Check before every request:** read [GET /usage](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-reference/endpoint/usage). Require Researcher/free, plan limit at most 1,000, zero pay-as-you-go usage and a zero or explicitly null pay-as-you-go limit, valid numeric account limits and key/account usage, and a ten-credit buffer. Unknown fields, paid plans and enabled overages stop the optional call. The app never changes billing settings. A dedicated key/account avoids other apps changing billing concurrently; the provider account's disabled paid usage remains the ultimate billing boundary.
- **Local budget:** atomically reserve two credits before each basic search or single-page basic extraction. A shared per-key lifetime ceiling of 400 reserved credits allows at most 200 attempts, including failures. No automatic monthly renewal or refund of uncertain requests; six attempts/minute globally. This deliberately leaves room below the advertised free allowance. The displayed app reservations are not a live provider balance.
- **Search:** at most five results restricted to the submitted official company domain; retain at most three approved URLs. Discard search content/answers. Company and person research must fetch and validate original pages before using any fact. Search runs only when ordinary discovery needs help.
- **Extract:** one official HTTPS URL, basic depth, markdown only, used when Reader fails. An unrelated returned URL is rejected. Known blocked company responses and explicit direct-page robots denials do not trigger this extra extraction route. Company identity, role matching, source quotes and score rules remain unchanged.
- **Limits:** page extraction still may fail or be incomplete. This is not a people database and cannot establish buying intent. Exa, Apollo, PDL and Firecrawl remain disabled; their availability alone does not verify a particular account has zero paid exposure.
- **Verification:** mocked API contracts and a real local D1 database cover paid/unknown-plan rejection, quota exhaustion, concurrent reservations, retained failed reservations, exact-domain extraction, no-key behavior, private URL-only discovery caching and secret handling. Provider coverage still requires broader evaluation; connecting a key is not a coverage benchmark.

[Published credits and endpoint costs](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-credits): 1,000 free monthly credits, basic search one credit, basic extraction one credit per five successful URLs. Our per-request reservation is intentionally more conservative.
