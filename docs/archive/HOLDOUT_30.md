## What the baseline exposed

The configured Jina app allowance was exhausted. Direct research recovered some pages, but the old 30-page per-workspace daily cap constrained the batch, while conservative extraction missed explicit operating statements. The application cap is separate from the provider token balance. No cap was reset, and no provider settings were changed.

Fixes add an early allowance check, explicit research mode, a 90-page bounded daily direct-company allowance, clearer direct-limit failures, better page selection, and narrowly expanded extractive classification. A/B/C priority remains separate from company fit.

The final-rules replay uses the exact archived live evidence and new classification rules, with no new retrieval. It is explicitly development evidence. It corrects the customer-versus-vendor false positive and two missed self-descriptions. Archived email drafts are not regenerated by this replay.

## Remaining limitations

The final replay leaves 11 unresolved: seven cases returned no usable company pages, and four returned pages without sufficient accepted operating-model evidence. Matched public roles rose from 2/9 to 3/9. These are material coverage limitations. The replay's zero wrong labels across 19 classifications is not 100% system accuracy: 11 of 30 still lack a classification, the set informed fixes, and email would-send ratings are still pending.

The next validation step is an untouched company/contact set after improving bounded official-page discovery and identity resolution. Do not relax attribution rules simply to hit a target percentage. The free Reader app allowance stayed at zero before and after the live recheck; provider token balance was not measured. Research remained available through direct public sources with unchanged safety checks.
